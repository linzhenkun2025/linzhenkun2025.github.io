About

About Me

I am an Agent Post-training Engineer working on the training and evaluation of capable, reliable LLM agents. My interests sit at the intersection of post-training, agentic reinforcement learning, and the systems that make long-horizon agents easier to improve.

I received my M.Eng. in Computer Science from Sun Yat-sen University, where I focused on natural language processing and text generation. Before moving into agent post-training, I worked on large-scale recommendation systems.
